# A plan's findings before its confirmation (`pkg/ui/flow`, `pkg/output`)

> **Unit:** refactor plan slice 2 (DNS, the pilot of the full flow), unit (c), its first part, `next/s2-flow-review`, on `next/s2-config-edit`. **Slice 2's decision 5, adopted by the maintainer on 2026-10-22:** the findings before the confirmation, "in the review, as the maintainer's warning lines of 2026-10-21 (`- ` in `status.warn`, DEV-060), each in a few words" ([gallery-dns.md](gallery-dns.md) §4). It builds the slot [confirm-cleanup.md](confirm-cleanup.md) §2.12 and §7 and [gallery-engine.md](gallery-engine.md) §7 left for P-41's best-effort warning, for every flow. New deviation: DEV-063; DEV-048 and DEV-060 amended. No V-number.
> **Status:** built and tested on macOS, in process: the flow on fixture engines at every cell of `uitest.Full()`, the flow adapter on the real engine over the recording fake (`hostfake`), the gallery's scenarios on its simulated hosts. No real terminal and no host were used; nothing here runs a command. Nothing is wired into `cmd/hardn` or the live main menu. **One line is new, proposed:** a udhcpc hook named by its path (§5). *Since the unit's review fixes* (§10): a finding whose detail shows nothing is reported after the flow; the hook's words; the record of `Human`'s change corrected.

## 1. Why

Until this unit `pkg/ui/flow`'s Plan step had no place for a finding, so `output.AfterFlow` printed a plan's findings after the result, as a Warning callout with each code and source: "dhcpcd may rewrite /etc/resolv.conf on a lease (DNS_DHCP_CLIENT, /usr/sbin/dhcpcd)" under "Operation Successful", and under "Operation Cancelled" after a decline. A warning read after the change is applied cannot change the user's answer. The DNS mocks drew the decided place, the review's last lines, from data of their own.

## 2. Guarantees

### The flow (`pkg/ui/flow`)

1. **`flow.Plan.Findings`**, new: what the plan found that stops nothing, each a `flow.Finding{Text, Code, Severity}`, in the order found. `Severity` is `SeverityWarn` (the zero value, so a finding is never drawn as less than a warning by default) or `SeverityNote`; any other value draws as a warning.
2. **Listed in the review, before the plan and its question.** Each finding is one more row of the review's section, a `KeyValue` bullet item: a warning's bullet and words in `KeyValue.ValueWarn` (`status.warn`, DEV-060), a note's words muted (`ValueNone`, DEV-061), wrapped under itself at col 6. A review with no rows, or no review, has no section; the findings are then a `BulletList` of their own in its place, after the notice when there is one, set apart by `SectionGap` (DEV-063). With no findings nothing changes: every flow without findings draws as before (`flow/*` goldens unchanged).
3. **The Engine plans before the review is drawn**, so that the review, its findings with it, is one block in one write (§9.10's rule). What the user sees is in the same order as before: review, plan, question. A context done while planning now ends the flow with nothing written, where the review was written before it.
4. **Every path that reaches the plan lists them**: applied, declined (`n`, `0`), a dry run (the plan is its output), a failure at a step, a typed confirmation, and a plan with no changes, before "Nothing needs changing." (P-41's warning is on every plan, one with nothing to change included). **A plan that cannot be made lists none** (`Engine.Plan`'s error is shown as the `ErrorReport`, as before).
5. **No code on the line** (decision 1, §3).

### The adapter and the report after a flow (`pkg/output`)

6. **`FlowEngine.Plan` hands the flow the plan's findings** (`reviewFindings`): the document's (`planFindings`, factored out of `Build`, its order unchanged: the plan's own, P-41's, then each change's in the plan's order), each once by its words and code, which is what its line says (a finding two changes carry is one line; decision 7), each `SeverityWarn`, as the human report words every finding, its line the finding's `Detail`, its code kept in `Finding.Code`.
7. **`AfterFlow` does not repeat them.** `Run.Reviewed`, new, says the flow listed the plan's findings; `FlowEngine.Run` sets it when `Plan` handed over a plan (no error, no refusal) and the flow reached a result, or ended at the confirmation (`q`, the end of the input, Ctrl-C), which comes after them. `AfterFlow` then leaves out every finding of the plan that the flow lists, each whose detail shows as a line (`listedFindings`, the flow's own test: a `KeyValue` bullet item that renders nothing is not drawn), and reports only what the flow did not list: after an apply, a decline, a dry run or a failure, nothing (the engine's `Result` carries no finding of its own; the failure's next step, D3's "Run resolvectl dns to see the servers in use.", is the flow's `ErrorReport`'s, as before). The countdown is unchanged.
8. **A refusal at the plan**: the flow shows the `ErrorReport`, lists no finding, and `AfterFlow` reports the plan's findings after it, as warnings with their codes and sources, as before (decision 4, §3). **A flow that ended before listing them** (a context done as it planned, a write that failed, a typed confirmation the flow cannot ask) is taken not to have listed them, so `AfterFlow` reports them. **A finding whose detail shows nothing** (empty, blank, or escape sequences alone, which the components strip) is no line of the flow's (`flow.Finding`: a finding with no text is not listed), so `AfterFlow` reports it, by its code and source, whichever the outcome; no producer gives one today, and `engine.Finding` does not require a detail. So a finding is shown twice at worst, never missed. **One `FlowEngine` serves one run of a flow**: it keeps the last plan it made, so a second flow on it that ended before planning would be reported with the first's plan, as before this unit; the gallery and the tests make one per run, and its doc says so.
9. **The human report says a finding once** (`findingsBlock`): a finding with the same detail, code and source is one line, whether two changes of a plan carry it or two controls' checks do (ssh's and the firewall's `SSH_NOT_IN_EFFECT`, both with source `ss -Htln`), in `Human` and `AfterFlow` alike; the line names no control, so nothing it showed is lost, and the JSON document keeps one per change or control. Another source is another line.
10. **The JSON document is unchanged**: `findings[]` holds every finding as before, one per change that carries it, with its control and change; `Reviewed` changes nothing in it (`TestAfterFlow` compares the two documents). `Human`, for a run no flow drew, changes only as §2.9 says, a repeated finding said once: it has no confirmation to list them before, so its findings stay after the plan, the steps or the status list, with their codes and sources (decision 6).

### The DNS mocks (`pkg/ui/screen`)

11. **The mocks' findings are their fixture plans'** (`flow.Plan.Findings`, with the control's codes), no longer rows of their review, so the flow's own slot draws them: every mock golden is byte for byte as before (`workflow-step/dns-*`, `play/dns*`), which is the check that the real flow draws the decided look. `TestDNSPlansAreReal` holds each sequence's findings, text and code, to what `output.FlowEngine` hands the flow on the gallery's resolver model, and the real ones to the plan's changes' findings.

## 3. Decisions of this unit

None is the maintainer's; each is for review.

1. **The code is not on the line.** README §9.15: a line says what happens in the fewest words, in the user's terms, "and leaves the exact lines to what shows them in full (the plan's diff, the journal, `--output json`)"; decision 5 says "each in a few words", and the decided mock shows none. The code stays in the JSON document (`findings[].code`) and the audit log, and on the human report of a run no flow drew; `flow.Finding.Code` carries it for a caller. *Alternative:* the code as a faint aside after the words, as a failed step's code is in its row (`StepProgress`'s detail, `Step.Detail` in `text.faint`), which needs a bullet item with an aside (a `KeyValue` change) and puts hardn's identifier on the line before the question.
2. **Plan, then the review.** The review is drawn once the plan is made, so that it is one block, its findings its last rows, as the mock draws it; drawing the review first and the findings under it after planning would split one block into two writes. A slow plan now shows nothing until it is made, where it showed the review first.
3. **With no review, no heading.** The engine's scenarios have no review, so their findings are a list of their own above the plan, with no section header: a header would be words no decision has, and the lines read as warnings by their colour, as DEV-060 records, and by their words without it.
4. **A refusal at the plan keeps the findings after its report**, with their codes: the flow has no plan to show, `Engine.Plan`'s error being its result, and a finding listed above an "Operation Failed" that refuses the plan would read as the refusal's cause. *Alternative:* the flow lists a refused plan's findings above the `ErrorReport` too (an `Engine` contract change).
5. **Every finding is a warning** (`SeverityWarn`), as `pkg/output` words every finding today; `SeverityNote` exists so that a lesser finding draws as the muted dash line decided on 2026-10-21 ("`text.muted` otherwise", DEV-061), and no finding is one yet. Which findings are less than a warning is the finding's owner's (its control's or the engine's), when one is.
6. **`Human`'s findings stay where they were**, changed only in that a repeated finding is said once (§2.9), across changes or across controls' reports; the JSON document keeps one entry per change or control. It renders a run no flow drew (a dry run or an apply without a terminal, once §3.11 wires them), where there is no confirmation to list the findings before; its plan is a log as much as a review, so its findings stay with their codes and sources after it.
7. **Each finding once, by its words and code** (`flow.Finding`'s fields), which is what its line says: two findings with one detail and two codes would be two lines (none does today); two with one detail, one code and two sources are one line, their sources left to the JSON document. DNS's udhcpc hooks were such a pair: each was "a udhcpc hook may rewrite /etc/resolv.conf" (`DNS_UDHCPC`), its path in its source alone, so a host with two hooks had one line, and, the flow having listed it, neither path, nor that there were two, on the screen. Each hook is now named by its path in its words (`steps.DNSFinding`, proposed, §5), as a DHCP client is by its name, so two hooks are two lines (`TestCheck`'s two hooks of one name, in two directories, where a base name would merge them again). A finding whose producer words several alike names what tells them apart. *Alternative:* the source in the key, two lines in the same words.

## 10. Review fixes

The review of 2026-10-22, each in its section above.

- **A finding whose detail shows nothing was shown nowhere** once `Reviewed` was set: the flow drew no line for it and `AfterFlow` left out every finding of the plan. `AfterFlow` now leaves out only those the flow lists, each whose detail shows as a line on the same `Env` (`listedFindings`), and reports the rest by code and source (§2.7, §2.8; `TestFlowEngineFindings`). The engine is not made to refuse a finding with no detail: a host's own message can be one, and refusing the plan for it would block a change over a warning's words.
- **Two udhcpc hooks were one line, with no path anywhere** (decision 7): each hook is named by its path (§5, proposed).
- **"`Human` unchanged" was not so**: §2.9, §2.10 and decision 6 now say that `Human` says a repeated finding once, across changes or controls' reports, and that the JSON keeps each.
- **A reused `FlowEngine`** reports an earlier flow's plan when a second flow ends before planning (as before this unit): recorded, and the type's doc says one serves one run (§2.8). Not changed: no caller reuses one.
- **P-41's proposed shorter line still named hardn**: proposed again, the host alone (§5), not built.
- **Without colour, a finding with no review reads like a plan change**: not changed, the look being the maintainer's call; listed for the maintainer before slice 8 (§8), and in DEV-063.
- **Doc sentences**: output.md §2.11's "which the flow's `PlanView` has no place for", `flow.Plan.Findings`' "The Plan step lists them" (the review's block draws them), `TestBestEffortRendered`'s comment; gallery-dns.md decision 5's note now says that the built slot reads the plan's changes, so a plan with no change lists none (§4), an input to the rest of unit (c). The first commit's body ("The Plan step lists each finding once"; the adapter dedupes, not the flow) is history, not rewritten.
